# Straitwatch (td_cargo_ship)

## Working with the player on design rounds

For a larger design or planning round, the default hand-off is a **review page**:
- a brainstorm with many ideas;
- a plan with open questions;
- specs or numbers to confirm before a build.

The page is a published HTML artifact where the player marks each item, writes notes and answers the questions. The next session reads the saved marks back. Follow the `review-page` skill (`.claude/skills/review-page/SKILL.md`).

Skip it for small asks (one or two questions, a single bug, a quick tweak): ask those in chat.
