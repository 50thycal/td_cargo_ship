## How the new enemies join the game

- **By chapter**, like the weapons (`src/v3/data/foes.ts`). A map in chapter *n* can send any enemy from chapters 1 to *n*. The chapter's own new ones come up most, and each map of the chapter leads with the next one in turn.
- **How many:** one a wave on waves 2 to 5, two on 6 to 9, three on wave 10 (more in endless). Each takes the place of a drone swarm in the wave, and the heavier ones (the platforms that fire several times, the ones that bring a heavy) a missile too, so a wave's weight stays close; the sweep checks it.
- **Surfaced** as the weapons are: the briefing lists the chapter's new enemies; the first time one shows up, the "new threat" card names it and says what to do.
- **Free Play** gets an Enemies line: as the campaign (the map's chapter), none of the new ones, all of them, or one on its own (it comes every wave, more often), to try each.
- **The rule for anything you can't intercept** (Rocket Truck's rockets, Siege Battery's shells, the Dive Bomber's bomb): a tell first, and the source can always be shot.

## Chapter 1: Home Waters (4)

Easy to read at a glance: each one bends a rule you already know (drones, missiles, trucks).

### Sprint Drones

*Strikes · Swarm.*

- **How it plays:** A swarm of four small drones flies in like the usual drones. Near its ship each one flares a booster for half a second, then sprints straight at it at four times the speed.
- **The tell:** The booster flare.
- **The answer:** Pick them off before the flare; Flak, Cutter and Scattergun take the swarm.
- **Numbers (first proposals):** 1 hit each. Cruise 70, sprint 300 from 240 px out. 1 damage.

### Decoy Drones

*Support · Deceive.*

- **How it plays:** A swarm of six that looks like any other, but three are decoys. Within 300 px of the convoy the decoys give themselves away: a white strobe and a dotted trail instead of the red light and exhaust. Decoys that reach a ship fizzle out harmlessly.
- **The tell:** Strobe and trail, up close.
- **The answer:** Wait and pick the real ones, or spend the rack early. Your helpers ignore decoys once they show.
- **Numbers (first proposals):** 1 hit each. Real ones do 1 damage; decoys none.

### Hydra Missile

*Strikes · Trick.*

- **How it plays:** A two-hit missile with a forked tail. The hit that kills it splits it into two fast darts aimed at the two ships nearest it. Killed far out, the darts are easy; killed close, they arrive before you can answer.
- **The tell:** The forked tail.
- **The answer:** Kill it early, or with an area weapon that catches both darts.
- **Numbers (first proposals):** Missile: 2 hits, 2 damage. Darts: 1 hit, speed 290, 1 damage.

### Rocket Artillery Truck

*Platforms · Shore.*

- **How it plays:** A truck drives down a shore road, stops, and slowly raises its launcher (2.4 s). Then it fires a rocket every 1.5 s, five in all, too fast to intercept. The truck itself is a target: four hits.
- **The tell:** The slow launcher raise.
- **The answer:** Drop everything and kill the truck while it raises.
- **Numbers (first proposals):** Truck: 4 hits. Rockets: speed 560, 1 damage, can't be shot.

## Chapter 2: The Narrows (4)

Each asks for a new kind of answer: a target that moves between spots, one that travels in the lane, a plate, a moment, a race.

### Attack Helicopter

*Platforms · Air.*

- **How it plays:** Flies in, settles over a firing spot beside the lane (the spot is marked as it comes), and fires a pair of small missiles every 3.2 s. After two pairs it moves to another spot; after six it leaves.
- **The tell:** It arrives and settles; the spot is marked.
- **The answer:** Commit six hits to it, or keep shooting its missiles (one hit each).
- **Numbers (first proposals):** Heli: 6 hits. Missiles: 1 hit, 1 damage.

### Torpedo Attack Submarine

*Platforms · Sea.*

- **How it plays:** A periscope, then the sub surfaces and stays up for 3.5 s, a target. It fires one slow torpedo along a straight line to where a ship will be, with the line drawn on the water. Anything in the line is hit. Then it dives.
- **The tell:** The wake line to the crossing point.
- **The answer:** Hit the sub while it is up, hit the torpedo (one hit), or change formation so no hull is on the line.
- **Numbers (first proposals):** Sub: 3 hits while up. Torpedo: speed 75, 3 damage (sinks a fresh ship).

### Armored Drone

*Strikes · Tough.*

- **How it plays:** A big drone with a plate on its nose. Shots from your gun that meet the nose hit the plate (four hits to break it). Halfway in it banks through a wide S-turn, and for that moment its side and tail face the carrier: shots then hit the drone itself (two hits). Blasts, the Railgun and the Lance go round the plate.
- **The tell:** The plate, and the bank.
- **The answer:** Time your shots for the turn, or use area weapons.
- **Numbers (first proposals):** Plate 4, body 2. Speed 60, 2 damage.

### Scavenger Drone

*Support · Sabotage.*

- **How it plays:** A drone with a claw that flies to the pickup nearest it (weapon, ammo, repair or Nuke), with a line to the one it wants. If it gets there the pickup is gone, and it flies off. With nothing on the water it waits at the top of the screen.
- **The tell:** The claw, and the line to the pickup.
- **The answer:** Shoot it first, or take the pickup before it arrives.
- **Numbers (first proposals):** 1 hit. Speed 170.

## Chapter 3: Dark Water (5)

Surprise and marks on the water. Two of the five are the stealth aircraft, on the chapter with the night map.

### Stealth Strafer

*Platforms · Air.*

- **How it plays:** A dark, faceted jet that comes in with no warning arrow and no band. It still flies 300 px onto the screen before its guns open, about a second to react.
- **The tell:** Only the jet, on its run-in.
- **The answer:** Shoot it on the run-in, or Shield and Aegis.
- **Numbers (first proposals):** 3 hits, as the Strike jet. Guns: 1 damage per hull.

### Stealth Bomber

*Platforms · Air.*

- **How it plays:** A flying wing (the B-2's shape, as the player asked) with no warning. After a long run-in it drops three guided missiles on the convoy. More armour than the missile bomber.
- **The tell:** Only the bomber.
- **The answer:** Spend six hits on it, or shoot its three missiles.
- **Numbers (first proposals):** 6 hits (the missile bomber has 3). 3 missiles, 1 hit each.

### Dive Bomber

*Platforms · Air.*

- **How it plays:** The player's redesign: a circle appears on the water where a ship is about to be. 2.2 s later the plane drops out of the sky onto it, and for 1.8 s it can be shot (enough to react and for a shot to fly there). If it lives, the bomb falls and nothing can stop it. The circle does not follow the ship.
- **The tell:** The circle, then the plane.
- **The answer:** Hit the plane in its window, or move the ships out of the circle.
- **Numbers (first proposals):** Plane: 2 hits. Bomb: 2 damage in the circle, 1 within 70 px.

### Coastal Siege Battery

*Platforms · Shore.*

- **How it plays:** A gun on the enemy shore. It marks two circles on the lane ahead of the convoy, and 3.4 s later its shells land in them. Shells can't be intercepted. It fires three salvos, 6 s apart. The gun can be destroyed.
- **The tell:** The circles on the water.
- **The answer:** Silence the gun, or move the convoy out of the circles.
- **Numbers (first proposals):** Gun: 6 hits. Shells: 2 damage each, 46 px.

### Target-Spotter Aircraft

*Support · Coordinate.*

- **How it plays:** A recon plane circles beside the convoy and paints one ship with a beam. While it paints, most new launches go for that ship, and 4 s in it calls a strike: three missiles from the bases at it. Shoot it down and the strike it called is cancelled; what has launched keeps coming.
- **The tell:** The beam on one ship.
- **The answer:** Break the spotter before the strike, or cover the marked ship.
- **Numbers (first proposals):** 4 hits. Leaves after 22 s.

## Chapter 4: Open War (4)

Support: enemies that change the rules for the others. Last, once every other enemy is known.

### Repair Tender

*Support · Shield.*

- **How it plays:** An enemy vessel that sails in with a heavy missile. Every 3.5 s it charges a beam (you see it for a second) and mends the most damaged enemy near it by two hits.
- **The tell:** The beam and its pulse.
- **The answer:** Finish the tough target between pulses, or sink the tender first.
- **Numbers (first proposals):** 6 hits. Mends 2 within 340 px.

### Bodyguard Ring

*Support · Shield.*

- **How it plays:** Three little guard drones circle a heavy cruise missile. A shot at the heavy meets a guard first: the guard dies, the heavy is untouched. Guards don't attack. Blasts that catch the ring clear it; the Railgun and Lance go straight through.
- **The tell:** The orbiting ring.
- **The answer:** Clear the ring with an area weapon, or spend three shots first.
- **Numbers (first proposals):** Guards: 1 hit each. The heavy as usual (8 hits).

### Lure Beacon

*Support · Sabotage.*

- **How it plays:** A flashing buoy in open water. While it flashes, your helpers (Drone Screen, Gunship Pair, Orbital Strike, Aegis, Frigate Escort, the allied guns) go for it instead of real threats, and their shots do nothing to it. Only your taps can sink it: two hits.
- **The tell:** The flashing buoy, and helpers turning toward it.
- **The answer:** Two taps on the buoy and your helpers are back.
- **Numbers (first proposals):** 2 hits (taps only). Gone after 20 s.

### Volatile Cloud

*Strikes · Trick.*

- **How it plays:** The player brought it back: a gas cloud drifts off the enemy shore and across the lane. Any explosion inside it sets it off (your bursts, enemy hits, a tap on the cloud). Set off over your ships, it hurts every hull under it; set off over open water, it's harmless. Left alone it thins out after 28 s.
- **The tell:** The cloud itself, drifting.
- **The answer:** Pop it before it reaches the convoy, and don't fire into it once it's over your ships.
- **Numbers (first proposals):** 1 hit pops it. 2 damage to each ship under it. Radius 75.

## Build order

1. **Phase 0, systems:** the roster by chapter, the waves mixing them in, the briefing and first-sight cards, the Free Play Enemies line, the play log.
2. **Phases 1 to 4:** one chapter's enemies at a time: the sim, the drawing, sounds, the bots, tests.
3. **Then:** the campaign sweep (each chapter's difficulty against the lock-in build), browser checks of each enemy on a phone, the docs.

## Test Lab round 1 (2026-11-08)

What the player's Test Lab verdicts (`tools/v3/test-lab/playtest-2026-11-08.json`) changed. Screenshots: `docs/design/img/enemy-lab-round1/`.

### Decoy Drones (tune)

*"Make the decoy drones a little less obvious, especially when they first launch... a little delay."*

- **The tell comes late.** A decoy strobes only within **160 px** of its ship (was 300) **and** not before **3.5 s** after launch (new, `FOE.decoy.revealAfter`). Measured on every map: they used to show from launch (as early as 0.02 s out) and for 4 to 6 s of a 7 to 10 s flight; now never before 3.5 s, and for about the last 2 to 3 s. A decoy that reaches its ship sooner than 3.5 s fizzles without a strobe.
- **Nothing else gives them away.** Decoys now fly exactly as the attack drones beside them (the same weave and nose swing; before, they flew dead straight while the real ones weaved), at the same speed, the same size and colour, the same launch and kill sounds, and now the same small kill burst (theirs was the bigger one). Helpers and bots still leave a decoy alone only once it has strobed.
- Shots: `decoy-1-launch*.jpg` (six alike), `decoy-2-reveal.jpg` (one strobing near its ship, the rest still alike).
